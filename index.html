About

Operations discipline, applied to product and brand.

I've spent over ten years in healthcare recruiting and staffing operations: sourcing, screening and placing medical professionals at national scale. At AMN Healthcare that led into a Product Owner role on a proprietary recruiting platform, where I owned requirements and the backlog and turned stakeholder input into user stories that shipped.

Today I'm a recruiter at Professional Therapy Partners. I built its first in-house recruiting function, and I keep building products on the side. Whether it's a candidate pipeline, a product backlog or a customer journey, I ask the same question: where's the friction, and what's the smallest system that removes it?

  • Systems thinking

    Every process (hiring, brand, product) is a pipeline with inputs, bottlenecks and conversion points.

  • Builder mentality

    I ship a working MVP solo (React, Node, Python, PostgreSQL) to prove an idea before investing more in it.

  • AI agents, with guardrails

    I design agent systems that take real actions behind approval gates, spending limits and audit logs.

  • Brand & positioning

    I've built identity systems from scratch: naming, color and type, voice, and lead magnets.
