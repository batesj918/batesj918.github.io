Live Product Build & Brand Positioning

Plastic Reset

The Problem
Most people know microplastics exist, but they have no idea what their own exposure looks like and no reason to change anything. Knowing about a problem in general doesn't change behavior or lead to a purchase. A personal, concrete number does.
The Solution
I built Plastic Reset end to end, starting with the brand: a faceted water-drop mark, a Navy/Blue/Green palette, Montserrat type, and a plain-spoken voice. The product sits on top of that:
  • A 12-question Microplastic Exposure Score quiz that scores users from 0 to 100 across 7 categories and produces a shareable score card. The quiz has a web version and a React Native companion app.
  • Results link straight to a curated shop of low-plastic swaps. Each product is tagged to a quiz category, and all product images are hosted on the site itself.
  • An email-gated 7-Day Plastic Reset Guide that uses double opt-in I built myself and feeds a Brevo email sequence.
  • A live news strip that refreshes every hour from Google News. If that feed goes down, it switches to publisher RSS feeds and a saved Postgres cache.
Tech / Tools
React/Vite, Expo (React Native), Express 5, PostgreSQL + Drizzle, OpenAPI codegen, Brevo transactional email, Canva (brand kit, logo, eBook), affiliate commerce.
The Impact
Live at theplasticreset.com. Real users take the quiz and share their scores, the shop routes each person to the products that fit their results, and every email subscriber is verified. I designed, built, and maintain all of it on my own.

Live Web Product — Gaming

tracksprites.com

The Problem
Fortnite's Sprites are collectibles with multiple variants and mastery levels, and it takes a lot of in-game “dust” to max them out. Each season adds new sprites and variant tiers. Epic provides no tool to track a collection, estimate costs, find codes, or coordinate trades, so players were stuck with spreadsheets and Discord threads.
The Solution
I built TrackSprites, a mobile-first, installable PWA that has grown from a single tracker into a full companion app:
  • A collection tracker covering 218 variants over two seasons, with a live dust-value breakdown by variant tier.
  • An interactive sprite map, a strategy guide, and a Lobby Hack code checklist.
  • Compare, a Squad Trade planner for 2–4 players, and shareable snapshot links.
  • Season and all-time leaderboards and friend groups, with sign-in through Discord, Google, or Epic. The site runs in 4 languages.
Operations
Most of the upkeep runs on scheduled AI agents. One checks every day for new codes and sprite variants and only publishes a change once two sources confirm it. Another posts lore and tips to the Discord and Facebook communities twice a day from a JSON content bank. When the Loot Hacker variant tier showed up in the game, it was live on the site the same week.
Tech / Tools
React + Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Express 5, PostgreSQL + Drizzle, OpenAPI/Orval codegen, OAuth (Discord/Google/Epic), Workbox PWA, i18next, pnpm monorepo, Claude Code scheduled agents.
The Impact
Live on its own domain, tracksprites.com, with a real player community. Top collectors on the leaderboard are tracking 200+ variants each, and the site keeps up with each season's content without me doing the updates by hand.

PT Survival Kit

The Problem
Physical therapists are burning out — buried under documentation, mandatory continuing education (CEU) requirements, and the pressure to stay current on evidence-based practice. As a recruiter hiring PTs day to day at Professional Therapy Partners, I see this firsthand.
The Solution
Built The PT Survival Kit, a one-time-purchase digital resource with three components: a Burnout Planner for setting boundaries and protecting time, a CEU Finder for locating affordable, relevant credits fast, and Evidence Briefs — short, readable research summaries with ready-to-use clinical talking points. A conversion-focused landing page carries visitors from pain point to solution to a low-friction purchase.
Tech / Tools
React + TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, Vite, Radix UI / shadcn/ui, Gumroad for checkout.
The Impact
A fully built, mobile-responsive landing page — one Gumroad listing swap away from being ready to sell.

Trading Bot

The Problem
Manually tracking and acting on trading signals is slow and emotional. The goal was a systematic, disciplined way to test trading strategies without a human in the loop making impulsive calls.
The Solution
Started as a Replit automation MVP and has since grown into a much more disciplined, multi-phase paper-trading system — now past Phase 26 — that combines options-flow data with congressional trade disclosures (STOCK Act filings) to generate signals, with full paper-only order submission and a daily report-to-dashboard pipeline in active development.
Tech / Tools
Python, Replit, Alpaca API (paper trading), options-flow & congressional-trade data feeds, automated email reporting.
The Impact
A verified, test-covered paper-trading system (448/448 tests passing) with confirmed brokerage connectivity, moving into hardening for always-on deployment — a long way from the original single-script automation it started as. In parallel I'm building Francis, a Python orchestration agent that connects to Robinhood through the official MCP connector and trades from a dedicated cash account. Its guardrails include a confidence threshold, a per-trade dollar cap, and a buying-power check.
