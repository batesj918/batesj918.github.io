No Ego Club

A philosophy content brand built on repeatable Canva templates and two long-form lead magnets.

Role
Solo: brand, content
Status
Brand asset
Platform
Canva · Metricool
Channels
FB · IG · TikTok · YouTube

The "the EGO …" series: one visual system, dozens of variations.

The challenge

Philosophy and self-improvement content lives or dies on consistency. A content brand needs a visual system it can produce at volume, and something deeper than a quote graphic to turn viewers into a community.

What I built

A full content system: repeatable template series mass-produced with Canva Bulk Create and scheduled through Metricool across four platforms.

Two long-form lead magnets pair Stoic and Buddhist ideas with a daily structure: the 10-page Ego Reset Workbook and a 12-page philosophy eBook.

Results

  • 75+ designs across several recognizable series.
  • One template becomes a full batch of on-brand posts.
  • Two lead magnets that give casual viewers a reason to come back.

Built with

  • Canva
  • Canva Bulk Create
  • Metricool
  • Long-form design
  • Short-form video scripting

Features 9 total

Content system

  • "the EGO …" series (controls, performs, judges, clings, compares, isolates and more)
  • "You are not …" series (your thoughts, the storm, the voice, your story and more), built in numbered bulk batches
  • "Ego vs Soul" and "Ego Death" templates, each creating 20–25 posts per bulk run
  • Hook-first vertical video templates and daily fact frames
  • Consistent brand kit: type, overlay treatment, @NoEgoClub signature

Lead magnets + distribution

  • The Ego Reset 7-Day Workbook (10 pages): Morning Primer, Physical Anchor, Daily Directive, Evening Review
  • Kill Your Ego, a 7-day philosophy practice eBook (12 pages)
  • Mixed scheduling in Metricool: auto-publish for standard posts, notification mode for TikTok and Instagram posts that need finishing in the native app
  • Distribution across Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube
